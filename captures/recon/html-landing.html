Invest likeit's 2021.

The market is frozen on 30 June 2021. Your team researches a hundred real companies, builds a portfolio for someone who is counting on you, then fast-forwards five years to see what it was really worth.

Enter the market →How it works

01 — The rule

Everything left of the lineis fair game. Everythingright of it is the exam.

Most investing lessons are taught with hindsight, which makes everyone a genius. Here you only get what an investor actually had on that Wednesday in June: the accounts, the ratios, the charts up to that day. Then the next five years happen to your portfolio, whether you saw them coming or not.

02 — How a round works

Five steps, start to finish

  1. 01

    Form a team

    Sign up with your own email. One of you starts the team; everyone else joins with its six-letter code.

    Two to five players

  2. 02

    Meet your client

    A draw hands you someone to invest for, with their own budget, worries and timeline. No swapping.

    Drawn at random

  3. 03

    Do the reading

    A hundred real NSE companies, exactly as they looked on 30 June 2021. Seven years of accounts, the ratios, the price history. Nothing after.

    The longest step

  4. 04

    Place your orders

    Spend the budget however you like, across as few or as many companies as you trust. Every teammate sees the same order book, live.

    Shared, live

  5. 05

    Fast-forward five years

    The seal breaks. Your portfolio runs to June 2026 against the Nifty 50, and you find out what your reasoning was worth.

    Scored out of 10

Half what it earned.Half whether it was rightfor them.

Fundamentals

50%

How good your companies looked on paper in June 2021, weighed for your client. Dividends matter to a retired couple and barely at all to a 22-year-old.
